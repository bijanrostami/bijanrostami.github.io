About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Toronto, working in the Communications and Networking Group under the supervision of Prof. Elvino S. Sousa. I received both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Electrical Engineering from the University of Tehran, where my graduate studies focused on communication networks. My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and communication systems, with a strong emphasis on learning-driven wireless and IoT networks. I actively work on deep learning and reinforcement learning–based approaches for next-generation communication technologies, including variational autoencoders (VAEs), transformer models, and self-supervised learning for semantic representation, resource allocation, and task-oriented communication.

Research Interests

  • Machine Learning for Communication Systems
    Deep learning, reinforcement learning, variational inference, transformers, self-supervised and representation learning.
  • Wireless Communications
    MIMO systems, interference management, ultra-dense networks, power control, user scheduling.
  • Information Theory
    Information bottleneck, semantic communications, joint source–channel coding, task-oriented communication.
  • Statistical Signal Processing
    Detection and estimation, MIMO detection, learning-based signal processing, high-dimensional inference.
  • Edge Intelligence and Distributed Inference
    Edge computing, multi-user and multi-task inference, energy-efficient and scalable learning systems.
  • Cyber-Physical and Smart Grid Systems
    Communication-aware control, IoT-enabled monitoring, data-driven optimization in power and energy networks.
